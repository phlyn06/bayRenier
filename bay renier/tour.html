About

Hi, I’m Mark Renier Cutanda.

For 4 years I’ve been guiding visitors around Alicia Panoramic Park, sharing the views, the stories and the best spots most people miss.

Along the way I picked up drone and camera work, so I can guide you and capture the moment at the same time. No need to hand your phone to a stranger.
